# Beyond Meat (BYND): Q2 2025 Earnings Brief

*Quarter ended June 28, 2025; reported August 6, 2025. Source: company earnings release only. Figures marked "derived" are our own calculations from the release.*

## Headline

**A weak quarter that keeps getting weaker, while the balance sheet is now the main story.** Revenue fell 19.6% to $75.0M on an 18.9% volume decline. Gross margin fell 320bp to 11.5%. Operating loss widened to $38.8M (-51.8% margin). Net loss narrowed slightly to $33.2M ($0.43/share), but only because of $5.7M of other income, mostly FX gains. Q3 revenue guidance of $68–73M points to a further drop from Q2. Management is accelerating cost cuts: a 6% workforce reduction and an AlixPartners restructuring partner named interim Chief Transformation Officer. Cash was $117.3M against $1.2B of debt.

## Key numbers vs. Q2 2024

| ($M, except per share) | Q2 2025 | Q2 2024 | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Net revenues | 75.0 | 93.2 | -19.6% |
| Gross profit | 8.6 | 13.7 | -37% (derived) |
| Gross margin | 11.5% | 14.7% | -320bp |
| Operating expenses | 47.4 | 47.6 | -0.4% (derived) |
| Loss from operations | (38.8) | (33.9) | +$4.9M worse |
| Operating margin | -51.8% | -36.4% | -1,540bp |
| Net loss | (33.2) | (34.5) | $1.3M better |
| EPS | (0.43) | (0.53) | $0.10 better |
| Adjusted EBITDA | (26.0) | (23.0) | $3.0M worse |
| Adj. EBITDA % of revenue | -34.7% | -24.7% | -1,000bp |

- **Identified charges of $9.2M:** $1.7M in COGS for winding down China operations, plus $7.5M in opex ($4.5M non-routine SG&A, $2.5M co-manufacturer arbitration legal costs, $0.5M partial HQ lease termination).
- **Adjusted for those charges (derived):** gross margin was about 13.7%, opex about $39.9M and operating loss about $29.6M (-39.5% margin). The release does not quantify comparable year-ago items, so the like-for-like comparison is approximate.
- **The EPS improvement is mostly dilution, not performance.** Implied average shares were about 77M, up from about 65M (+18–19%, derived).

## Channel trends

| Channel ($M) | Q2 25 | Q2 24 | YoY | Volume | Rev/lb |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| U.S. retail | 32.9 | 44.9 | -26.7% | -24.2% | -3.2% |
| U.S. foodservice | 11.1 | 10.4 | +6.8% | +2.3% | +4.4% |
| Int'l retail | 15.9 | 17.6 | -9.8% | -13.1% | +3.9% |
| Int'l foodservice | 15.1 | 20.4 | -25.8% | -21.6% | -5.3% |

- **U.S. retail drove about two-thirds of the $18.2M revenue decline (derived).** Causes were weak category demand, lost distribution points and heavier trade discounts. Its share of revenue fell to 44% from 48%.
- **International foodservice drove about 29% of the decline.** QSR customers bought fewer burgers.
- **U.S. foodservice was the only channel that grew**, on ground beef and dinner sausage, but it is just 15% of revenue.
- **International retail held up better than the other declining channels.** FX and pricing partly offset weakness in Canada and the EU.
- **Unit economics worsened (derived).** COGS per pound rose about 3% while revenue per pound fell 0.9%. The release cites a higher inventory provision and materials costs, spread over lower volume.

## Cash and debt

- **Cash including restricted cash: $117.3M. Total debt: about $1.2B,** roughly 10x cash and about 4x annualized revenue (derived).
- **First-half cash flow:** operating cash burn was $59.4M (vs. $47.8M a year ago) and capex was $6.4M (vs. $2.5M). Free cash flow was about -$65.8M (derived), or about $33M per quarter.
- **Funding:** financing inflows of $33.6M included a $40M initial draw on the Delayed Draw Term Loan from Unprocessed Foods, LLC (an affiliate of Ahimsa Foundation).
- **Runway (derived):** at the first-half burn rate, cash covers about 3.5 quarters before any further financing.

## Guidance

- **No full-year guidance**, citing elevated uncertainty.
- **Q3 2025 net revenue of $68–73M.** The $70.5M midpoint is about 6% below Q2's revenue (derived).
- **Workforce reduction:** about 44 North America roles (about 6% of the global workforce). One-time cash charges are $0.8–1.3M, mostly in Q3. Expected savings over 12 months are $5–6M in cash compensation plus $0.5–1.0M in non-cash stock compensation.

## Analysis

- **The cost base still doesn't fit the revenue base.** Even excluding charges, opex of about $40M is 53% of revenue, while gross profit is about $10M. Annualized, the adjusted EBITDA run-rate is about -$104M (derived).
- **The RIF helps only at the margin.** It saves about $1.3–1.5M per quarter, roughly 4–5% of Q2's adjusted operating loss (derived). Much deeper cuts or a return to volume growth are needed. Neither is in sight given the guided Q3 decline.
- **Net loss quality is poor.** Without the $6.3M swing in other income, which came mainly from FX gains, the net loss would have been worse than a year ago.
- **The balance sheet is the key risk, and also the key event to watch.** With $1.2B of debt against $117M of cash and about $33M of quarterly burn, the following all point to a recapitalization that may come soon and could be heavily dilutive for equity holders:
  - the restructuring hire;
  - the CEO's stated goal of "strengthening our balance sheet";
  - the risk-factor language on exchanging the convertible notes for equity, lender warrants and "significant dilution".
- **Bottom line:** fundamentals are still deteriorating across the two largest channels. We would focus less on quarterly operating results and more on the terms of any debt restructuring.
