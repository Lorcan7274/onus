## Guidance and cost actions

- **Outlook.** No full-year guidance. Q3 2025 net revenue is guided to $68-73M (midpoint $70.5M, about 6% below Q2 on a sequential basis, derived). The release gives no margin, EBITDA or cash guidance.
- **Reduction in force (approved Aug 6).**
  - About 44 North American employees, about 6% of global headcount.
  - One-time cash cost of $0.8-1.3M, mostly in Q3.
  - 12-month savings of $5.0-6.0M cash plus $0.5-1.0M non-cash stock compensation.
  - Payback is about 2-3 months. Savings are only about 5% of annualized Q2 Adjusted EBITDA loss (derived), so this is insufficient on its own.
- **Governance and restructuring.**
  - Expanded AlixPartners engagement.
  - John Boken (AlixPartners Partner and Managing Director, turnaround and restructuring) appointed interim Chief Transformation Officer.
  - Management is pursuing opex cuts "to fit anticipated near term revenues", focus on core product distribution, and margin initiatives.
- **Other actions.**
  - China operations suspended and substantially ceased ($1.7M COGS charge in Q2).
  - Partial termination of the El Segundo campus HQ lease ($0.5M).
  - Ongoing arbitration with a former co-manufacturer ($2.5M incremental legal in Q2).

## Credit view

The credit is weak and deteriorating.
- **Revenue and margins.** Revenue is falling about 20% year on year with no guided stabilization. Gross margin is thin at 11.5%, or about 13.7% excluding the China charge. Operating margin is -51.8%.
- **Cash burn against a large debt stack.** Burn of about $33M a quarter against about $117M of cash gives under a year of runway, and the debt is about $1.2B. Cash covers about 10% of debt. At this scale, equity-funded or operating solutions look insufficient. Any resolution probably requires a restructuring of the converts, such as exchange or conversion, with dilution.
- **Cost cuts are too small.** The RIF saves about $6M a year, against Q2 opex of $47.4M (about $40M excluding charges) and a gross profit of only $8.6M. Opex is about 4.6x gross profit (derived). Break-even needs a very large cost or margin change.
- **Support.** The delayed-draw loan and an interim restructuring officer show some lender and sponsor-style support. The $40M initial draw covered only about 1.2 quarters of burn (derived). The remaining draw capacity is not disclosed in the release.
- **Positives.** U.S. foodservice growth, H1 net loss helped by FX, and a fast-payback RIF. None changes the trajectory.
- **Watch items.**
  - Q3 revenue against the $68-73M guide.
  - Quarterly cash burn.
  - Remaining delayed-draw capacity.
  - Note exchange or refinancing terms.
  - Further dilution and impairments.
