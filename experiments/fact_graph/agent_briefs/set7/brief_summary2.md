# Beyond Meat (BYND) Q2 2025 Results: Brief Summary

**Source:** Revised Q2 2025 earnings release dated August 8, 2025 (quarter ended June 28, 2025). The revision corrects SG&A only. Figures below come from the narrative text; the release's tables were not present in this extract.

**Headline:** Net revenue fell 19.6% to $75.0M on an 18.9% volume decline. Gross margin fell to 11.5%, and the operating loss widened as a share of sales. The net loss narrowed, helped by a $6.3M swing in other income.

## Results vs. year-ago quarter (Q2 2024)

| Metric | Q2 2025 | Q2 2024 | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Net revenues | $75.0M | $93.2M | -19.6% |
| Gross profit | $8.6M | $13.7M | -$5.1M (-37.2%)* |
| **Gross margin** | **11.5%** | **14.7%** | **-3.2 pts** |
| Operating expenses | $43.5M | $47.6M | -$4.1M (-8.6%)* |
| Loss from operations | $(34.9)M | $(33.9)M | $1.0M larger (+2.9%)* |
| **Operating margin** | **-46.6%** | **-36.4%** | **-10.2 pts** |
| Other income (expense), net | $5.7M | $(0.6)M | +$6.3M |
| Net loss | $(29.2)M | $(34.5)M | $5.3M smaller (-15.4%)* |
| Net loss per share | $(0.38) | $(0.53) | |
| Adjusted EBITDA | $(22.1)M | $(23.0)M | -29.5% vs. -24.7% of revenue (-4.8 pts) |

\*Derived by this summary from the stated figures.

## Channel performance

| Channel | Q2 2025 revenue | YoY | Volume | Net rev/lb | Share of revenue (Q2 2024) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| U.S. retail | $32.9M | -26.7% | -24.2% | -3.2% | 43.9% (48.2%) |
| U.S. foodservice | $11.1M | +6.8% | +2.3% | +4.4% | 14.8% (11.2%) |
| International retail | $15.9M | -9.8% | -13.1% | +3.9% | 21.2% (18.9%) |
| International foodservice | $15.1M | -25.8% | -21.6% | -5.3% | 20.1% (21.9%) |
| **Total** | **$75.0M** | **-19.6%** | **-18.9%** | **-0.9%** | 100% |

U.S. retail was the largest driver of the decline, with weak category demand and fewer points of distribution. Its ingredient sales fell to $0.1M from $0.8M. U.S. foodservice was the only channel that grew, on higher ground beef and dinner sausage volume and price increases. International foodservice declined on lower QSR burger volume.

## Charges in the quarter

- **Total: $5.3M** (matches the release's "$5.3M in expenses" for the net loss).
- Cost of goods sold: $1.7M, China suspension and cessation of operations.
- Operating expenses: $2.5M incremental arbitration legal expenses (former co-manufacturer dispute); $0.6M non-routine SG&A; $0.5M partial lease termination at the El Segundo Campus Headquarters.

## Cash, debt and financing

- Cash and equivalents (including restricted cash) at June 28, 2025: **$117.3M**. Total debt: **about $1.2B**.
- Six-month operating cash outflow: $59.4M, up from $47.8M. Capex: $6.4M, up from $2.5M.
- Six-month financing inflow: $33.6M, including an initial $40.0M draw on the Delayed Draw Term Loan with Unprocessed Foods, LLC (affiliate of Ahimsa Foundation), net of issuance costs.

## Guidance and cost actions

- **Guidance:** No full-year guidance. Q3 2025 net revenue is expected at **$68M to $73M**.
- **Reduction in force:** About 44 North America roles (about 6% of global workforce), approved August 6, 2025. One-time cash charges of $0.8M to $1.3M, mostly in Q3 2025 but possibly later. Expected savings of $5.0M to $6.0M in cash compensation over 12 months, plus $0.5M to $1.0M in non-cash stock compensation.
- **Leadership and advisers:** Expanded AlixPartners partnership. John Boken appointed interim Chief Transformation Officer.
- **Strategy:** Faster opex reduction, prioritizing core product distribution, and margin expansion on core products. Opex ex-charges fell mainly on lower marketing and selling expense.

## Derived figures

- **Gross margin ex-China costs:** ($8.6M + $1.7M) / $75.0M = **13.7%**, versus 14.7% a year ago (-1.0 pt). This assumes no comparable China cost in the year-ago quarter.
- **Operating margin ex-disclosed charges:** ($34.9M - $5.3M) / $75.0M = **-39.5%**.
- **Opex ex-disclosed charges:** $43.5M - $3.6M = **$39.9M**, versus $47.6M reported a year ago. The year-ago figure may also contain items that were not disclosed here.
- **Revenue mix:** U.S. $44.0M (-20.4%); international $31.0M (-18.4%). U.S. retail accounted for $12.0M of the $18.2M total decline.
- **Net debt:** about $1.08B ($1.2B debt less $117.3M cash).
- **Six-month cash burn incl. capex:** $65.8M, versus $50.3M a year ago. The run rate is about $33M per quarter. At that rate, $117.3M covers roughly 3.5 quarters before any new financing. This is a rough estimate.
- **Financing bridge:** $40.0M draw less $33.6M net financing inflow implies about $6.4M of other financing outflows, which the narrative does not itemize.
- **Q3 guidance vs. Q2:** Midpoint of $70.5M is -6.0% sequentially (range -9.3% to -2.7%).
- **Implied headcount:** 44 roles at 6% implies roughly 730 global employees. This is approximate.
- **Other income:** Mostly net realized and unrealized FX transaction gains, per the release.

## Data limits

- The channel, volume, charges and financial statement tables were stripped from this extract, so the table values above are taken from the narrative text.
- The corrected SG&A figure is not stated separately in the narrative. The $43.5M total operating expenses figure is used instead.
- Channel revenue sums to $75.0M for Q2 2025 and to $93.3M for Q2 2024 (the release states $93.2M), a $0.1M rounding gap.
