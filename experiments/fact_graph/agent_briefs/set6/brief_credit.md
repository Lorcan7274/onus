# Beyond Meat (BYND): Q2 2025 Credit Note

**Source and scope:** Beyond Meat Q2 2025 earnings release dated August 6, 2025 (quarter ended June 28, 2025). This is the only source used. The channel and volume tables in the release were blank in the source file, so channel-level volume analysis is not included. Figures marked "derived" are my arithmetic on release numbers. Nothing after August 2025 is reflected.

**Bottom line:** Negative credit view. Cash of $117.3M covers roughly 11 months at the first-half 2025 burn rate, against about $1.2B of debt. EBITDA is deeply negative, so leverage ratios are not meaningful. Debt service depends on refinancing, note exchanges or new equity, and the release flags each of these as possible.

Revenue fell in every channel except U.S. foodservice (+6.8% to $11.1M). U.S. retail fell 26.7% to $32.9M, international foodservice fell 25.8% to $15.1M, and international retail fell 9.8% to $15.9M. The release cites weak category demand, reduced U.S. retail distribution and lower QSR burger sales.

The net loss improved only because of $5.7M of other income, mostly FX gains. The operating loss worsened by $4.9M.

## Charges (Q2 2025)

- Cost of goods sold: $1.7M from the suspension and substantial cessation of China operations.
- Operating expenses: $4.5M non-routine SG&A, $2.5M arbitration legal costs (former co-manufacturer dispute), $0.5M partial lease termination at Campus Headquarters.
- Total identified charges: $9.2M (derived). Excluding them, the operating loss is about $29.6M (derived). The release does not give year-ago comparable items.
- Not quantified: higher inventory provision and possible future impairments of Campus Headquarters space.

## Liquidity and leverage

- **Cash:** $117.3M including restricted cash at June 28, 2025. The release does not split out unrestricted cash.
- **Debt:** "$1.2 billion" total outstanding (rounded in the release). Net debt is about $1.08B (derived). Cash covers about 10% of debt (derived).
- **Burn (six months to June 28, 2025):** Operating cash outflow $59.4M (vs. $47.8M). Capex $6.4M (vs. $2.5M). Operating cash plus capex was $65.8M (vs. $50.3M), up about 31% (derived). That is about $11.0M per month (derived). Quarterly cash flow is not disclosed separately.
- **Financing:** Net inflow of $33.6M, including an initial $40.0M draw on the Delayed Draw Term Loan with Unprocessed Foods, LLC (an affiliate of Ahimsa Foundation), net of issuance costs. Investing outflow was $6.1M.
- **Runway:** $117.3M / $11.0M per month is about 10.7 months (derived), or roughly mid-2026 from the balance-sheet date. This assumes no new financing and no reduction in burn. Restricted cash is included, so unrestricted runway is shorter.
- **Leverage:** Q2 Adjusted EBITDA annualizes to about -$104M (derived), so debt/EBITDA is not meaningful. Debt to annualized revenue is about 4.0x ($1.2B / $300M, derived).
- **Coverage:** Interest expense is not disclosed in the release, so interest coverage cannot be computed.

## Guidance and cost actions

- **Guidance:** No full-year guidance. Q3 2025 net revenue is expected at $68M to $73M, a midpoint about 6% below Q2 (derived). Q3 2024 revenue is not in the release, so a year-over-year comparison is not possible.
- **Reduction in force:** About 44 North America roles (about 6% of global workforce). One-time cash costs of $0.8M to $1.3M, mostly in Q3 2025. Expected savings are $5.0M to $6.0M in cash compensation over 12 months, plus $0.5M to $1.0M of non-cash stock compensation. Cash savings equal about 4% of the annualized H1 burn (derived), so they do not change the runway picture on their own.
- **Other actions:** Interim Chief Transformation Officer (John Boken, AlixPartners) appointed. The company is accelerating opex reductions, prioritizing core products and pursuing margin programs. Campus Headquarters space is being surrendered, subleased or terminated, with possible impairment.

## Credit view

The credit profile is weak. The capital structure is large relative to cash and operating scale. The release names the main risks:

- Refinancing of the convertible senior notes, and their repurchase or cash settlement obligations.
- Exchanges of notes for equity, with significant dilution and possible loss of NOLs.
- Warrants under the Unprocessed Foods loan and facility, and potential tax on cancellation-of-debt income.
- Use of the at-the-market equity program.

The release does not provide maturities, covenants, collateral terms, interest costs or credit ratings, so those are not assessed here. Any exchange below par or equity-for-debt swap would be a material credit event for holders.

**Watch list:**
1. Q3 2025 revenue against the $68M to $73M range.
2. Cash balance against an H1 pace of about $11M per month.
3. Any note exchange, repurchase or new term-loan draw.
4. ATM equity issuance.
5. Realization of RIF savings and Campus lease outcomes.
