# Beyond Meat (BYND): Q2 2025 KPI Datasheet

**Source:** Beyond Meat revised Q2 2025 earnings release (Exhibit 99.1, dated August 8, 2025), narrative-only text. Quarter ended June 28, 2025.
**Revision note:** The company reissued this release only to correct "Selling, general and administrative expenses" and how that change flows through the statements of operations, balance sheet and cash flow statement. All figures below come from the revised text.
**Conventions:** $ in millions unless stated. "Reported" means the release states the figure. "Derived" means I calculated it from reported figures. Change percentages are the company's where it gives them; otherwise I calculated them. Small differences come from rounding to $0.1M.

**Data gaps:** The source file is narrative only. It does not include the channel revenue tables, the volume-by-channel table, the line-by-line charges table, the non-GAAP reconciliation tables or the financial statements. Because of that, it has no pound volumes, no line-by-line D&A or stock-based compensation, no company-reported Adjusted loss from operations or Adjusted net loss values, and no year-ago one-off charges.

| Volume of products sold | n/a | n/a | -18.9% | Reported % only |

| Operating margin | -46.6% | -36.4% | -10.2 pp | Reported |

| Net loss per common share | $(0.38) | $(0.53) | +$0.15 | Reported |

| Adjusted EBITDA % of net revenues | -29.5% | -24.7% | -4.8 pp | Reported |

Notes:
- Volume and price explain the revenue decline: 0.811 x 0.991 = 0.804, or -19.6%.
- The operating loss widened because gross profit fell. Lower OpEx (marketing and selling) offset part of that.
- Net loss improved only because of the FX-driven swing in other income. Operating performance got worse.
- COGS per pound rose because of higher inventory provision and materials costs, partly offset by lower manufacturing (including depreciation) and logistics costs.
